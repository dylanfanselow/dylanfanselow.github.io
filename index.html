Basketball-RPG

A side-view pixel-art basketball career game with RPG progression. Play 5v5 team basketball where you have to earn the ball. The CPU coach picks plays from a simulated "play lab", defenses key in on ball hogs and learn your moves, and a story mode follows the trials and tribulations it takes to cement yourself in basketball history.
